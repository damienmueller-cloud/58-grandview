Luxury, sophistication & effortless functionality

Perfectly positioned in one of New Lambton Heights’ most sought-after pockets, this exceptional family residence has been completely transformed to an uncompromising standard. Every detail has been thoughtfully considered, with premium fixtures and finishes showcased throughout.
